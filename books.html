Books & Translations

पुस्तक तथा अनुवाद

I am involved in translating Christian books and theological resources into Nepali and Hindi. My aim is to make sound biblical teaching accessible to readers who may not otherwise have such resources available in their own language.

The books and resources available through this website may be downloaded and used for personal reading, theological education, discipleship, and ministry purposes. However, printing, republishing, reproducing, distributing for profit, or selling any of these materials is strictly prohibited without prior written permission from the copyright holder. Users must also follow any additional copyright and usage conditions stated with each publication.
