Sermons & Bible Studies

प्रवचन तथा बाइबल अध्ययन

This section presents sermons, Bible studies, devotional messages, and teaching materials prepared for church services, house fellowships, seminars, and personal study. The aim is to explain Scripture clearly and encourage faithful Christian living.

यस खण्डमा मण्डलीका आराधना सेवा, घरेलु सङ्गति, गोष्ठी तथा व्यक्तिगत अध्ययनका लागि तयार गरिएका प्रवचन, बाइबल अध्ययन, आत्मिक सन्देश तथा शिक्षण सामग्रीहरू प्रस्तुत गरिन्छन् । यसको उद्देश्य धर्मशास्त्रलाई स्पष्ट रूपमा व्याख्या गर्नु र विश्वासयोग्य ख्रीष्टियन जीवनका लागि उत्साहित गर्नु हो ।

Running the Race by the Rules

Hebrews 12:1-3 • 2 Timothy 2:5 • 1 Corinthians 9:24-27

A sermon on running the Christian race according to God’s rules: faithful church attendance, love for believers, Spirit-filled living, confessing Christ, holy living, and carrying the Gospel to the whole world.
