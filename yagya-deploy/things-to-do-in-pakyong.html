Pakyong travel guide

Planning your time in Pakyong

Planning a stay in Pakyong? Use Yagya Farms as a relaxed base and build your Sikkim itinerary around local scenery, slow travel and nearby experiences.

Keep the itinerary realistic

Sikkim travel is more enjoyable when the plan leaves room for road conditions, weather and unplanned stops. Build each day around a small number of priorities rather than trying to cover too much.

Make time for the property itself

At Yagya Farms, the pool, outdoor spaces and mountain setting are part of the stay. Leaving an afternoon free can be more rewarding than turning every day into a long road itinerary.

Ask your host for current local guidance

Local conditions and access can change. Before heading out, ask Pramila for practical guidance relevant to your dates and route.

Stay at Yagya Farms

Yagya Farms is a small homestay in Dikling, Pakyong with mountain views, a swimming pool and direct host contact. For current room availability and rates, contact Pramila Karki on +91 95477 92455 or WhatsApp Yagya Farms.
