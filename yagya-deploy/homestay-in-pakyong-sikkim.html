Pakyong accommodation guide

Choosing a homestay in Pakyong, Sikkim

Looking for a homestay in Pakyong, Sikkim? Yagya Farms offers mountain views, a swimming pool, comfortable rooms and direct local hospitality.

Why travellers choose Pakyong

Pakyong can suit travellers who prefer a quieter base and a more personal connection with the place they are visiting. A small homestay makes it easier to speak directly with the host about practical arrival details, meals and local travel.

What to compare before booking

Look beyond price alone. Check the real location, room facilities, host communication, parking if you are driving, food arrangements and whether the property’s atmosphere matches the trip you want.

Why Yagya Farms stands out

Yagya Farms combines guest rooms with mountain scenery, green surroundings and a swimming pool. It is positioned for travellers seeking a relaxed stay rather than a large hotel environment.
