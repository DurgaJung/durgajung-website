Welcome to My Official Website

Ministry • Bible Teaching • Translation • Technology

I am Durga Jung Kunwar, an ordained Elder at Bahrain Nepali Church. Through ministry, Bible teaching, translation, theological study, and software development, I seek to strengthen believers and provide practical resources for churches and Nepali-speaking Christian communities.
